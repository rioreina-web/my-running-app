What each one costs you

Same screen, very different bills. The right-hand column is the one to read carefully — it's the work that lands after the palette swap feels good.

A note on the accent token. Every direction above still routes its accent through the token named coral, even when it's yellow or cyan. That's deliberate — the name describes the role, and renaming it would touch 972 call sites for zero visual gain.

And on mixing. These are positions, not packages. Swiss layout with the brutalist accent, or technical density on paper instead of black, are both legitimate outcomes — pick the bones from one and the voice from another.

What happens next

Once you name a favourite — or a hybrid — this is the path from a picture to something running on your phone.

Step 1

Build the switch

Ship DripTheme with both themes set to identical values. The app should look completely unchanged — that's how you know the plumbing works before any design is riding on it.

Step 2

Pour in the palette

Drop the winning hex values into DripColors.wild, flip the setting in Settings, and the whole app recolours at once. About 60 lines, one file, 5,959 call sites.

Step 3

Rebuild the Log screen

Add VoiceLogViewWild.swift beside the original and gate it on the theme. The old screen is never touched, so it can't break while you work.
