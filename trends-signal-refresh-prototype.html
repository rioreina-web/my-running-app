Your layout, on the rebrand palette — off-white #FAFAF9, cool ink #0D1016, one scarlet #E63946. Three windows: 14D day by day for the close look, 1M a month of days, 3M bucketed to weeks for the overview. Every lane changes resolution with the window. What changed

  1. Three windows, pinned at the top like TrendsWindowPicker. 14D is the deep look — full-size day cells, every night of sleep. 1M is the same marks across a month. 3M is the overview.
  2. 3M buckets to weeks. 91 daily columns in 262px is 2.9px each — the marks stop carrying anything. At 3M every lane switches to weekly and the chart footer says so. It also means 3M hides a single bad night; if that matters more than the overview, one constant moves it back to daily.
  3. Miles per week is the whole week. No more 48* / 10*. A finished week shows its full total even where the window cuts through it — the block just runs off the left edge. The week in progress shows actual solid, planned above it with a dashed cap, labelled with the projected total.
  4. The gutter fits its labels. 46px — no more VIGGLE and VK VOL.
  5. Each lane captions its own scale underneath: PEAK 96, USUAL 6H20–8H, USUAL 43–47 BPM.
  6. Sleep and RHR are lines over your usual band, not deviation bars. Nights outside the band get a scarlet dot and a value.
  7. Chips fit on one line, same fill-on / hairline-off language, scrolling with a fade.
  8. The day readout stops printing twice. The card below is the real detail — and at weekly resolution it becomes the week.
