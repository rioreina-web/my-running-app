What changed, and why

Trends · simplified · sample data from the May–Aug block

The screen answers before it reports

Every section now leads with a sentence in plain English, then shows the chart that proves it. The old screen stated facts — 50, 64, 75, 0.93 — and left you to work out what they meant. Numbers still carry the weight, per the voice rules, but they sit inside a claim.

The three stat cards became one chart

This week, 4-week average and peak are all readable off a single 12-week bar chart: current week in coral with the projection dashed above it, the average as a dashed rule, the peak labelled where it happened. You get the three numbers and the shape — the down weeks, the 3:1 rhythm — which the cards could never show. The numbers repeat once underneath as a quiet footer row.

ACWR is a word now, with the number as a footnote

"Balanced · 0.93" instead of a bare ratio on a grey track. The state is what a runner acts on; the ratio is evidence.

The pace histogram became a stacked bar

Twenty-three pace bins is a shape you have to study. One 342px bar split easy / steady / quality, with your zone boundaries labelled underneath, is a shape you read in a second — and it surfaces the actual finding, which is that a third of the block sits in the grey zone.

Two new sections earn the open

02 · What's actually changed is the fitness proof — threshold minutes rising while band pace falls. That's the section that makes the tab worth opening on a Tuesday. 04 · Best of the block is the reward: three things you did that were hard, written the way you'd tell a training partner about them.

One shared 12-week axis

Sections 01 and 02 use the identical x-axis, so scrolling reads as the same twelve weeks through a second lens. Key-session ticks sit under the load bars, which is the only place load and quality currently touch.

Six tabs became four

Trends, Charts and Sheet were three flavours of the same thing. Folding Charts and Sheet behind "Everything else ›" at the bottom of Trends gives the tab a job: the block, summarised. Ask takes the freed slot.
