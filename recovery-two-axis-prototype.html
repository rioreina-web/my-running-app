Two axes, never added together. Load says what training is asking; Body ranks today's signals against your own history, so 50 is your own median day and every band stays reachable. Your words lead, the watch corroborates. Today's run counts tomorrow.

What changed in the logic. The old card summed demand and supply into one 0–100. That made a 39 ambiguous (big block feeling fine, or barely running feeling awful?), put the score on a fiction of a scale (real range 8–77 drawn on a 0–100 gauge), and let a missing channel lower the roof until Clear was arithmetically unreachable — which the card then had to apologise for in four clauses of 9pt uppercase.

Now. Load is the Banister difference in real units. Body is today's supply channels ranked against this athlete's own trailing 180 days, so 50 is their own median, both ends are reachable by construction, and a missing channel narrows confidence instead of lowering a ceiling. The headline is the quadrant — computed on every ledger since 6 Aug and never once rendered on this tab.
