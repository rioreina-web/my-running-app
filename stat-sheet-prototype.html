Three decisions this draws out

One row is one day, not one upload. Aug 4 is five Strava activities for one track workout. Tap it: the five pieces are inside, the threshold rep at 6:02 clearly separated from its warm‑up and cooldown. Across all your data, 253 upload rows are 174 days run.

Pace is computed, never read. workout_pace_per_mile is populated on 31 of 253 rows. Duration is there on 249. So the column is derived from distance and time, and the blue ramp is anchored to the range on screen rather than to fixed thresholds, which is how PaceSpectrum works.

Quality is weight, not colour. Threshold, interval and race days are set in bold ink; filler is ink‑2. Coral appears once in the whole screen, on the flag that tells you a duplicate row was folded into a total. Open Jul 18 to see it.
