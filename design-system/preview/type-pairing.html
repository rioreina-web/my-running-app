Rules

Italic mono is the athlete; roman mono is the machine. Nothing else is set in mono.

Crimson never carries a label or a number. If it is scanned rather than read, it is Schibsted or Inter.

No licensed faces. If the budget ever appears, Haas Display goes back on --font-display and nothing else changes.

Times survives in exactly one role — the single-line italic dek, via --font-serif. Archivo has been retired.

Every face here is free to ship. Neue Haas Grotesk and Akzidenz-Grotesk were tried and removed — licensed Monotype and Berthold faces, and the only files available were unlicensed rips. Instrument Sans replaces Haas because it measured closest of any free grotesk (+3.1% at 46px/700, so line breaks hold); Schibsted Grotesk carries the labels, where its width does not matter. Crimson Pro and JetBrains Mono are self-hosted in fonts/; Instrument Sans, Schibsted and Inter load from Google.
