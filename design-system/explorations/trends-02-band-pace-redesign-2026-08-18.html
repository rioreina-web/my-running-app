Every chart above draws from exactly this table — the accessible twin.

One colour change runs through all three. The current chart draws band pace in coral. The design system’s three-palette rule says blue = pace, warm = mood, coral = alert — coral is never a pace fill. So pace moves to --pace-lt #27549B (the LT stop on the pace spectrum, which is literally the band this section measures), volume stays warm grey, and coral is spent only on “this is the latest session”. That also solves a legibility problem for free: the two series no longer differ by position alone.
