The headline system

Say what moved, not how they're doing.

The old headline was a verdict about the athlete. The new one is an observation about a number — so it can be generated by template, verified against the facts, and never flatters.

The line the copy can’t cross
KeepA signal name, a direction, a numeral, a period. “Load, back in band.”
KeepThe alert case first. A live niggle outranks any good news above it.
CutSecond person. “You’re…”, “your body…”, “you’ve been…” — the headline describes data, not Maya.
CutTrajectory promises. “coming back”, “on the up”, “turning a corner”, “bouncing back” — all forecast the future from 30 days of past.
CutPraise and consolation alike. “strong month”, “rough patch”, “great work” — observation over congratulation.
CutAny numeral not present in the fact lines. The template can only fill from computed values.

Net effect: the headline leaves the LLM entirely. What remains narrated is the one optional line beneath it — and the screen still reads correctly with that line switched off.
