What this is

The fourth direction: instead of a list (Ledger), a 12-week trend (Fitness Signal), or a grade (Execution & Load), this is one key session told as a story — what you ran, what your heart said, what the day cost you, what you said into the phone afterward, and how it sits against the last workout of the same kind.

Swipe the strip to move between the 21 sessions. Everything below the strip re-renders per session.

The ghost

The dashed line behind the rep bars is the previous comparable session — last intervals for intervals, last long/tempo for sustained work — aligned segment by segment. It answers "was that better than last time?" without a single extra number.

The comparison card beneath makes it explicit: raw pace, heat-neutral pace, effort HR, fade, drift — this time vs then, with the delta doing the talking.

The words are real

10 of the 21 sessions have actual voice-memo notes pulled from training_logs (cleaned_notes + mood), and the knee mentions on 24 May and 18 July come from body_mentions — shown as a NIGGLE pill. Sessions without a memo show an honest empty state, which itself is the nudge to record one.

How the variables show up

  • Heat — key pace is shown raw, with heat-neutral pace directly beneath it and the estimated cost in sec/mi in The Day Itself.
  • Elevation — climb and hilliness (m/km) sit next to heat, and a rolling-terrain hill cost (≈0.33 s/mi per m/mi of climb, net of descents) feeds the flat + cool pace so hills get the same honest treatment heat does. Estimated; production would use grade-adjusted pace from the streams.
  • HR drift — its own card under the HR track: decoupling for sustained runs, first-to-last-rep HR rise for intervals, against the <5% durable zone. Not scored on short reps, where drift is meaningless.
  • HR at the fast segments — the coral track under the pace bars, same x-axis, own scale (never a dual axis).

Where this lands

Tap-through from the Ledger row or from a workout card. Engine is a pure function next to pace-heat-adjustment.ts in supabase/functions/_shared/; SwiftUI view in RunningLog/Trends/. The Read follows Coach voice: observation, numbers, soft question — no prescriptions.
