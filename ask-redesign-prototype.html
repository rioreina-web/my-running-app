Every figure on this screen was pulled from the live database on 31 Aug 2026. Nothing is invented. Earlier drafts of this prototype ran on fabricated numbers — a threshold-pace series that does not exist, 33–58 mile weeks against your real 52–77, and three calf quotes I wrote myself when the niggle on file is the knee. That is a bad way to judge a surface whose whole argument is that it shows its work.

So the fixture is now real, and where the data is thin the prototype says so on the card rather than filling the gap. Two metrics were dropped rather than faked: there is no stored threshold-pace series anywhere, and parsed_structure holds block distances with a null pace on every one, so rep paces are derived from mile splits and labelled as derived.

Direction I throughout: white stock, one red, hairlines instead of cards, tabular figures, 22px gutter. This file links design-system/direction-one/direction-one.css and hardcodes no colour.
