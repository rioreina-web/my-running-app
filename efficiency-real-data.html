What your real data settles

The verdict is “not yet”, and that is a real finding. Eight weeks and 16 quality sessions still leave a band of -1.4 to +2.8 points a month. Anyone shipping a confident arrow on this data is showing you noise.

Your best and worst sessions are two days apart. Aug 6 at 105.9 and Aug 4 at 97.1 — nearly nine points, from the same athlete in the same week. That spread is the honest scale of the thing, and it is why the band is wide.

What would change it. Roughly 25–30 quality sessions in a window brings the band near ±1 point. At your rate that is about three more months — or sooner if the 22 sessions without heart rate start carrying it.
