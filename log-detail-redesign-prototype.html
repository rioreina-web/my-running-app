What got cut

  • VOICE SUMMARY section — gone. It was an AI paraphrase of the memo sitting directly above the memo. Two voices saying one thing.
  • "Show full transcript ↓" → "Show all · 0:42 ↓". Same job, carries the audio length, stops shouting "transcript".
  • AI INSIGHT’s always-on 68-word paragraph → one 44pt button. Nothing generated is thrown away; it just waits to be asked.
  • "WORKOUT NOTES / OPTIONAL" → a single ＋ Add a note row. An empty labelled section is worse than no section.

What the memo becomes

  • Italic PT Serif, 17.5px, coral left rule — the same treatment your journal already gives a quote. It reads as a human speaking.
  • Clamped to 4 lines (A) or 2 lines (B). Never hidden behind a disclosure; you always see the opening.
  • Audio strip directly under it. The memo is a recording — playing it back should not require hunting.
  • Filler ("humid, humid day") stays. It's your voice. Only strip it in edit mode, if ever.

If the insight is worth a tap

  • Cap it at two sentences, then a number row. Right now it hedges for 68 words and lands nowhere.
  • Lead with the finding, not the framing. "Reps faded — 7th at 5:52" beats "This session shows a slight fade".
  • Drop the flattery. "A credit to your toughness" is the part that makes the whole block feel like filler.
  • Show the evidence as data (FADE −12s · DEW PT 72° · REC DRIFT 3/7) so the claim is checkable, not just asserted.
