What nulling it changed

The curve got much tighter. R² went from 0.910 to 0.952, and session-to-session scatter fell from 2.3 index points to 1.6. One session was adding almost a third of the noise.

It flipped the direction. With Aug 6 the trend read +0.4 points a month. Without it, −0.4. Both still cross zero, so neither is a finding — but a single bad heart-rate file was steering the story. Hit “Include it” above and watch the line tilt.

Your automatic gate would have caught this one. Scored against a fit that never saw it, Aug 6 lands at z = 4.75, past the 4.0 threshold, with the next-worst session at 1.67 — clean separation. The prototype had it in because my crude pre-filter only looked for heart rates under 150; the real algorithm in EfficiencyIndexModels.swift is smarter than my shortcut was.

Ship the manual control anyway. Athletes know when a strap misbehaved, and no gate catches everything. Excluded sessions should stay visible, say why, and be reversible.
