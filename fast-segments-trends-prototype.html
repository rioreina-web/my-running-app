What this is

The Fast-Segment Analyzer, rebuilt in Post Run Drip — warm paper, ink, one coral, Crimson Pro / PT Serif, pace on the blue depth ramp. It slots into the Trends tab (or Train ▸ History).

Same real data, same detection engine (segment-engine.js) — only the skin changed. Fast reps are isolated from your key sessions and trended by pace, volume, heart, density and heat.

Note on color

Pace uses your blue ramp (pale → navy = easy → fast), so darker reps are faster. Coral stays reserved for alerts, per the three-palette rule. Heat is a neutral gray magnitude, not a mood color.
