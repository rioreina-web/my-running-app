About the hills toggle

Your terrain barely varies. Across these 16 sessions elevation runs 17 to 55 ft per mile — a grade correction of 4 to 12 s/mi. Flip the toggle and no index moves more than 0.2 points.

There is a reason it nearly cancels. The index is your actual ground-per-beat against what the curve predicts at that pace. Correct the pace for hills and you also slide along the curve, so most of the correction cancels out of the ratio. It would matter for someone training at 150 ft/mi; in Austin it doesn’t.

Ship it anyway. It costs nothing, and your backend already computes a proper per-lap grade model (flatPaceSec, conditionsPaceSec) that is better than the ft-per-mile estimate used here.
