What both fix

Your words lead. Each row renders cleanedNotes ?? notes — the runner's own voice — instead of the AI's coach_insight. That one swap is what turns a stats feed into a journal.

Stats become caption. Distance, pace and type drop to a quiet mono line under the words, not the headline.

No AI annotation inline. Honors your own IA rule — Log is pure record; insight lives on Coach & Trends.

A vs B. A keeps the voice record button on the surface (habit loop, voice-first). B is a denser week-grouped logbook with recording moved to a chip — closer to a classic training diary.
