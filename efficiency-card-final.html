What changed underneath

BaselineWas: a 180-day curve containing the sessions it graded, so the number sat at 100 forever. Now: the earlier period is the baseline and recent sessions are measured against it — so the number can actually move.
UnitWas: “index 100” and “1.81 m/beat”. Now: heart rate at a pace you already know. Nothing to learn.
Paces shownWas: mile, 5K, 10K, HMP, MP as chips over a 28-dot scatter. Now: MP and LT, read off the curve at your ladder’s anchors. LT works even though no session is ever labelled LT.
When it speaksNew: only when the change clears ~4 bpm and its confidence interval clears zero. Otherwise it says “holding steady” and states the resolution.
Still to guardA heart-rate source change (wrist vs strap) can fake a 4 bpm swing and flip the sign. HealthKitManager already captures the source name — the card should refuse to compare across a switch.
