What moves

Four values in DesignSystem.swift, the same four in globals.css, and colors_and_type.css to keep the design folder honest. Roughly 25 lines across three files.

What the type costs

Colour alone reflows nothing. Swapping the display face touches ~32 dripDisplay callsites; dropping PT Serif touches every dripBody and dripCaption — about 900 hardcoded point sizes that will shift line breaks.

What still needs hunting

66 hardcoded colours in 14 Swift files and 250 in 21 web files bypass the tokens. They keep the old coral until someone walks them.
