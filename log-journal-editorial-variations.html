How each honors the stats

1 · Caption strip — stats become a labeled caption bar (type / dist / pace / mood) under the quote. Most scannable; the numbers form a clean ledger row without competing with the words.

2 · Ledger margin — distance is a big serif numeral in a left margin, pace + time stacked beneath. Feels like a training logbook; your eye can run the left edge for mileage.

3 · Article entry — type + distance ride in a serif headline, the rest sit in a refined figure line under an editorial rule. Most "magazine"; reads slowest and richest.

Shared: voice/text indicator top-right, mood as the single restrained color, generous serif body, and the record affordance still on the surface (Direction A).
