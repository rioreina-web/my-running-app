The three changes

  • Summary shrinks and trims. 15.5px ink → 13.5px warm gray, two sentences instead of three. It stops competing with your stats for the top of the visual hierarchy.
  • Transcript becomes a play row. 41 words of wall → one 44pt row: play button, waveform, duration. Words behind "Read the words ↓".
  • Insight becomes a button. ✦ Read the insight →. Opens inline, capped at two sentences plus an evidence row.
  • "WORKOUT NOTES / OPTIONAL" → a single ＋ Add a note row. An empty labelled section is worse than no section.

What the summary prompt has to change to

  • Two sentences, hard cap. Sentence one: what the session was. Sentence two: how it felt. Nothing else.
  • Drop "I ran a session with…" — the reader is the runner. Start at the content: "Steady, easy and fast reps on a hilly, humid course."
  • No hedging clauses. "Despite this, I felt healthy overall" → "knee held up fine."
  • At 13.5px, anything over ~30 words looks like a paragraph again. The type change only works if the prompt change ships with it.

Build notes — checked against your code

  • audio_url is already on training_logs and on the TrainingLog model, so the data is there.
  • But there is no AVAudioPlayer anywhere in the app yet — the play row is genuinely new UI, not a re-skin. Budget for a small MemoPlayer view.
  • Duration isn't stored. The 0:42 needs either a new column or reading it off the asset after load. A column is cheaper than a fetch on every row.
  • Some paths write a public URL (audioPublicURL) while the transcript loads from the private training-memos bucket. Playback needs the signed-URL path, same as VoiceTranscriptText already does.
  • generateCoachInsight() exists in the view model and is wired to nothing (see the comment at line 461). A button is its obvious home — generate on tap, not on every memo.
