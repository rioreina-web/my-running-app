What changed & why

  • One hero, not five peers. The 80/20 read is now the lead — display headline plus an italic standfirst that carries the number. The screen answers one question before you scroll.
  • Workout card → quiet link. The four-row white card is gone; the list lives one tap away behind “View all ↗”, with just the latest quality session teased. Removes the only card-on-paper on the screen.
  • Four stat tiles → a borderless three-up line. 76% Easy got promoted into the headline (it’s the story), leaving miles · runs · hours as a quiet hairline-divided line. No squeezed four-up row.
  • Segmenter moved to the top. The lens control (Week · Month · Block) sits right under the plate strip instead of floating mid-screen between stats and chart.
  • One supporting visual, with air. The intensity chart is the single chart, simplified to the Easy/Hard split that matches the “80/20” read, and given vertical room. Coral marks only the hard portion — the thing the read is about.
  • Back on-system. Editorial line · dot · line rules replace boxed sections; pace-zone labels (“5K”) replace the retired “Intervals / Tempo / Threshold”; the em-dash placeholder in the standfirst is gone; coral appears once per cluster.
