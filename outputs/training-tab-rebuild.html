What changed

  • Voice off. Removed every narrated line — “hard share is creeping,” “your volume piles up around 7:19…”. Training is now data + labels only. The coach voice belongs on The Read.
  • One tab, one scroll. Period stats → weekly volume → easy/hard → volume × pace → quality sessions → goals. Each section separated by an editorial rule, one idea each.
  • Interpretation became a number. Where a sentence used to explain the pace chart, there’s now a factual “MEDIAN 7:19 /MI” in the header instead.
  • Your colors kept. The pace gradient and the easy(sage)/hard(coral) split carry through; coral stays on the “hard” signal only.
  • Pace-zone labels. Quality sessions read 5K / MP / LT, not the retired “Intervals / Tempo / Threshold.”
