The line that's safe: facts and arithmetic only — counts, percentages, and real deltas (“+8% vs. May”). The line that's tacky: anything with an interpretive verb or judgment — “creeping,” “carrying the load,” “on track,” “strong month.” The moment a word implies an opinion about the data, it reads as the coach talking, and that belongs on The Read.
