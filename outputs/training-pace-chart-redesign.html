What changed & why

  • Gradient kept. The Easy→Fastest color ramp on the bars is untouched — that's the part you liked and it's doing real work (color = pace).
  • Three x-axis labels → one. The crowded EASY/MP/LT/5K row on top of the bars, the numeric time axis, and the separate legend strip all said the same thing. Now a single slim gradient axis sits under the bars and is the legend.
  • Quality paces as light markers. MP / LT / 5K become thin vertical reference lines on the chart with a small label, so you can see exactly where your spikes land — without a header row fighting the bars.
  • Headers untangled. “VOLUME × PACE · LAST CURRENT FITNESS · 30 DAYS · EXPAND ↗” was wrapping into itself. Now a clean eyebrow left, scope right.
  • Easy/Hard tidied. The 80/20 target moves to a quiet centered marker instead of competing coral on both ends; coral stays on the “hard” side only.
  • Editorial rules separate the two modules instead of raw whitespace, matching the rest of the system.
