Each just labels the view. None says whether it was good, bad, high or low — that judgment is what would make it read as AI. The headline could also follow the segmenter: “This week.” / “This month.” / “The block.”

The rule of thumb: a headline that names the screen is fine (“Your training.”). A headline that grades it is the tacky AI line (“Strong month.”, “Hard share is creeping.”). Keep the display type, drop the verdict. If you ever want a verdict, it lives on The Read.
