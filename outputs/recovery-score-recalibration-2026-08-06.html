04 · What changed in the code

One factor, one file. recentLoad in TrendsRecoveryFactors.swift now reads the 3-day decayed carried load against your own 8-week average daily load: at or near your usual → 0 · meaningfully lighter → +2/+4 · heavier → −4/−6/−8 · nothing in 3 days → +6 (unchanged) · under two weeks of history → the old absolute charge as fallback. The receipt line grows a personal reading — "21 mi over 3 days · about your usual" — still counting and stating, never prescribing. Factor maxima are unchanged, so theoreticalRange and the band-reachability guard hold as-is. Five new guard tests pin the recalibration (RecoveryRecentLoadRecalibrationTests), including "carrying your usual load scores zero."

Your verification: ⌘B, then ⌘U — watch the new suite. Day-to-day volatility rose slightly (4.25 → 4.79 mean move) as the factor now steps between personal thresholds; the ±3 noise gate in the daily delta already absorbs this on screen.
