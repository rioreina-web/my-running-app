What each turn costs in SwiftUI

This is the part that decides it. Three of these four are small additions to the pager you already have. One is a rewrite.

TurnReads asHow it's builtCost
Slide A carousel. Clean, but nothing says "page". What you ship today — .scrollTargetBehavior(.paging), untouched. Already done
Paper slide A magazine. The leaving page falls behind the spine. .scrollTransition on the existing pager — offset, scale and a gutter overlay. No new gesture code. ~40 lines, one file
Hinge A book. Unmistakably a turn, and the most memorable. rotation3DEffect anchored .leading, driven by a manual DragGesture — which means giving up .scrollTargetBehavior(.paging) and hand-rolling velocity, rubber-banding and the turn-lock. Rewrites the pager
Stack A deck of cards being dealt. Modern, app-like. .scrollTransition plus a z-index flip. Same mechanic as paper slide, different numbers. ~40 lines, one file

The one thing to fix first, whichever you pick. You have two pagers running in opposite directions. HistoryDetailPager is book order — oldest first, today last. HomeDayPager is newspaper order — today is the front page and turning goes back in time. Your own PAGED-HOME-APPLY.md flags this as open question §8.1. Once the turn looks like paper, that contradiction stops being a detail and starts being a bug people feel: back-in-time is left in one surface and right in the other.

The Sheet needs page breaks before it needs a turn. One month per spread, as you chose — August is a page, July is a page. It gives every page a headline and a footing total, and turning the Sheet becomes turning back through your training year.
