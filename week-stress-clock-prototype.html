Every run sits at the hour it actually started. Its width is how long it lasted, its height is what a minute at that pace costs, and its colour is the pace spectrum — so the area of every block is its training load score. A long easy run is a wide low slab. A 5K session is a narrow spike. You can compare them by eye because they are drawn in the same units.
