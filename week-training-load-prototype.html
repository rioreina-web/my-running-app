Post Run Drip · Prototype Week training load

The week, by load.

Each day's width is its share of the week's training load. Pie area is volume, colour is pace zone, and the dots below carry mood and niggles. Two weeks, same block: one with seven running days, one with five.

Load model mirrors TrendsQualityLoad.swift / _shared/workoutSegmentation.ts: load = Σ (minutes in zone × zone weight), weights easy 1.0 → mile 8.0. Colours are the ten PaceSpectrum stops. Pie radius scales as √volume so ink is proportional to miles. Widths are a share of that week's load, so read the wmin totals to compare the two weeks.
