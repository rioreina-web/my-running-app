The one idea

Five data streams, one shared x-axis. Most apps silo these into separate cards; the value is in the vertical alignment — read straight down any week and see mileage, the quality session, how she felt, and whether her body flagged anything, all at once.

The four tracks

Volume + intensity — bars are weekly mileage; the darker cap is quality miles, so intensity rides inside volume (no separate chart).

Key sessions · pace — each quality session as a coral dot; higher = faster. Coral earns its place here — these are the sessions that matter.

Mood — one dot per week, colored by the dominant feeling pulled from voice logs.

Niggles — a marker on any week a body part was mentioned. Surface, never diagnose.
