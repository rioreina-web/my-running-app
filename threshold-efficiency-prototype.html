Three things worth knowing about these numbers

LT works even though your sessions are never labelled LT. The backend folds threshold work into HMP, so no session carries an “LT” tag — but it doesn’t need to. The curve is fit across every pace you ran, then simply read at the LT pace from your ladder. Same for MP. That’s why this survives where the zone-by-zone version struggled.

The change is real, not noise. Jackknifing every session in and out, the MP change stays between −1.2 and −3.7 bpm and LT between −1.4 and −4.7 — the sign never flips. The old index card couldn’t clear that bar; this does.

It errs conservative. Spring was cool, July was not, and heat raises heart rate on its own. Your heart rate at both paces fell anyway, so the true gain is at least this big.
